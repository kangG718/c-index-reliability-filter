# C-index Reliability Filter

A model-agnostic framework that uses agreement between multiple XAI feature rankings as a reliability signal for filtering financial trading predictions.

## Current research position

The verified STEP=2 evaluation cache contains 861 rows across three models and 218 unique evaluation dates. At 0bp, the selected active C-index filters changed pooled per-trade results as follows:

| Model | Best filter | q | Baseline trades | Filter trades | Baseline Sharpe | Filter Sharpe | Delta Sharpe |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GBM | `C_RBO_full` | 0.5 | 62 | 33 | 0.1582 | 0.2762 | +0.1179 |
| MLP | `C_RBO_full` | 0.3 | 88 | 56 | 0.1436 | 0.1871 | +0.0434 |
| RF | `C_tau_ties3` | 0.4 | 176 | 106 | 0.1437 | 0.1999 | +0.0562 |

The positive direction remained under 5bp and 10bp transaction-cost assumptions. The C-index filters also outperformed the prediction-confidence filters across the model-cost pairs. However, none exceeded the 95th percentile of count-matched random selection, and no model-cost pair was statistically significant after 20-day block-permutation max-stat multiple-testing correction. The adjusted p-values ranged from 0.3548 to 0.7071.

The evidence is therefore exploratory: explanation consensus may be useful as a reliability-filter signal, but the current study does not establish statistically significant or universal performance improvement.

## Reproduction modes

### Quick downstream reproduction

Load `artifacts/finalDf_cache.pkl` and rerun the evaluation notebooks. Model retraining and XAI recomputation are not required for the transaction-cost, benchmark, or statistical analyses.

### Full reproduction

Rebuild the data, retrain all models, recompute XAI rankings and C-index values, and rerun the evaluation notebooks. Trained model weights, the MLP scaler and architecture metadata, and the full window-level XAI caches are not yet published, so full reproduction currently requires training from code.

An isolated rerun reproduced identical row keys, predictions, all six C-index columns, selected trade sets, and trade counts. The only observed difference was floating-point noise in `ret_1d_next` (maximum absolute difference `3.75e-12`); downstream Sharpe differences were below `1.2e-11` and did not change any reported conclusion.

## Main experimental settings

- Assets: SPY, TLT, and GLD.
- Data period: 2012-01 through 2025-12.
- Models: LightGBM, Random Forest, and PyTorch MLP.
- XAI methods: Permutation Importance and SHAP.
- Rolling window: 60 trading days.
- Main sampling step: 2 trading days.
- Filter quantiles: 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, and 0.5.
- Primary reporting: pooled per-trade Sharpe together with Trade Count.
- Statistical test: 20-trading-day block bootstrap/permutation with max-stat multiple-testing correction.
